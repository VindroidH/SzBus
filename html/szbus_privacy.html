隐私政策


I hereby state, to the best of my knowledge and belief, that I have not programmed this app to collect any personally identifiable information.

我在此声明应用程序没有收集任何个人身份信息。

Anonymous analytics data may be collected for the purpose of improving t he app, for example, identifying which function is particularly popular.

收集匿名分析数据可能是为了改进应用程序，例如，确定哪个功能特别受欢迎。

The only data stored locally on your device are bus route caches, which can be simply erased by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

本地存储在设备上的唯一数据是公交路线缓存，可以通过清除应用程序的数据或卸载应用程序来删除这些数据。

HTTPS Requests are made to the above data sources to retrieve bus routes, stops information. No personally identifiable information (apart from information essentially needed to perform a request, such as the IP address) is sent in such requests.

发出的HTTPS请求是用来检索公交路线、站点信息。在此类请求中不会发送任何个人身份信息（除了执行请求所必需的信息，如IP地址）。
